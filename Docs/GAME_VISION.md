# ArmyRush Vision Document

Version: 1.0
Status: Master Vision

---

# Purpose

ArmyRush is a mobile-first crowd combat runner inspired by the highly engaging onboarding gameplay popularized by Last War.

The goal is not to create a simple runner.

The goal is to create a highly polished, highly replayable, highly satisfying power fantasy where players continuously grow an army, overcome increasingly difficult encounters, and experience constant progression.

This document serves as the highest-level vision for the project.

When making decisions:

GAME_VISION.md takes precedence over all other documents.

---

# Mission Statement

Create a mobile game that delivers:

Immediate Fun

Clear Choices

Constant Growth

Massive Army Battles

Satisfying Destruction

Long-Term Progression

while remaining easy enough for anyone to understand within 30 seconds.

---

# Product Goal

ArmyRush should feel:

Fast

Rewarding

Accessible

Replayable

Polished

Premium

The player should feel stronger after every session.

---

# Player Fantasy

ArmyRush is fundamentally a power fantasy.

The player fantasy is:

"I am building an unstoppable army."

The game should constantly reinforce:

Growth

Scale

Power

Momentum

Dominance

Victory

---

# Core Experience

The player begins with a small squad.

Through smart decisions and strategic path selection, that squad grows into a massive army.

The player defeats enemies, destroys obstacles, defeats bosses, earns rewards, upgrades their army, and becomes stronger over time.

The experience should constantly alternate between:

Building Power

and

Spending Power

---

# Design Pillars

Every feature must support at least one of these pillars.

---

## Pillar 1: Readability

Players must instantly understand:

Good choices

Bad choices

Threats

Rewards

Progress

Never sacrifice clarity.

---

## Pillar 2: Satisfaction

Every interaction must feel rewarding.

Examples:

Gate selection

Enemy defeat

Obstacle destruction

Boss defeat

Level completion

Upgrade purchase

---

## Pillar 3: Growth

Players must constantly feel stronger.

Growth is the most important long-term retention mechanic.

---

## Pillar 4: Momentum

The game should rarely stop.

Players should spend most of their time:

Moving

Choosing

Fighting

Winning

---

## Pillar 5: Accessibility

Anyone should understand the game within:

30 seconds

No complicated mechanics.

No confusing systems.

# Control Philosophy

One-handed play.

One finger input.

No complex gestures.

No multi-touch requirements.

---

# Visual Philosophy

ArmyRush should look:

Clean

Colorful

Bright

Readable

Modern

The game should never appear:

Dark

Realistic

Military-simulator-like

Overly cluttered

---

# Audio Philosophy

Audio should reinforce:

Power

Impact

Progress

Reward

Every important action must generate feedback.

---

# Performance Philosophy

The game must maintain:

60 FPS

on modern iPhones.

Gameplay quality must never be sacrificed due to poor optimization.

---

# Retention Philosophy

Retention should come from:

Progress

Mastery

Collection

Goals

Not frustration.

Not artificial waiting.

Not excessive grind.

---

# Difficulty Philosophy

The player should feel:

Smart

Powerful

Capable

Difficulty should challenge decisions.

Not punish players unfairly.

---

# Monetization Philosophy

Monetization should never block progress.

The game must remain enjoyable without spending money.

Purchases should accelerate progression or provide cosmetics.

Not create paywalls.

---

# Production Quality Philosophy

ArmyRush is intended to feel like a commercial mobile title.

Not:

Prototype

Tech Demo

Student Project

Game Jam Entry

All systems must be polished before considered complete.
